Skin Examination Consent and Preferences - Males

Prepared by your family doctor
June 23, 2026

Why This Examination Matters

A complete skin examination is one of the most effective ways to detect skin cancer early, when it's most treatable. Melanoma and other skin cancers can appear anywhere on your body, including areas not typically exposed to the sun.1

Important Limitation

Even with dermoscopy (a special magnifying instrument), this examination cannot detect every skin cancer. You should report any new, changing, bleeding, or non-healing lesion to your physician promptly.2

Your Comfort and Preferences

Your comfort and dignity are important to us. Please review the following sections and indicate your preferences. You may withdraw or modify your consent at any time without affecting your care.

1 · Buttocks and Lower Abdomen

Melanoma can occur in these areas. If underwear is kept on, the physician will need to briefly adjust the fabric to view the skin underneath.3

2 · Inner Thigh

Examining the inner thigh requires adjusting the position of your legs.

What to Expect

  • The examination typically takes 10-15 minutes
  • Your doctor will examine your skin systematically from head to toe
  • A dermoscope may be used to look more closely at any concerning spots
  • You'll be asked to change positions to examine different body areas
  • Your privacy and comfort will be maintained throughout

3 · Final Consent

I have read the information above and understand the purpose of this skin examination. I understand that even with dermoscopy, this examination cannot detect every skin cancer, and that I should report any new, changing, bleeding, or non-healing lesion to my physician promptly. My preferences above will be respected throughout, and I may withdraw or modify my consent at any time without affecting my care.

After Your Examination

Your doctor will discuss any findings with you and provide recommendations for follow-up care if needed. Remember, most skin changes are benign, but early detection of any problems leads to the best outcomes.
