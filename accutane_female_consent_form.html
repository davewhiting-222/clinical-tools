Isotretinoin (Accutane) Treatment

Informed Consent and Pregnancy Prevention Agreement
For Female Patients of Childbearing Potential
This form must be completed, signed, and retained in the patient chart before isotretinoin is prescribed. A copy is provided to the patient.
Part 1 — Patient Information
Part 2 — Risk of Birth Defects

⚠️ ISOTRETINOIN CAUSES SEVERE BIRTH DEFECTS. This is the most critical risk of this medication. Even a single dose taken during pregnancy can cause major malformations, including abnormalities of the brain, heart, face, ears, and limbs, as well as miscarriage and stillbirth. There is no safe dose of isotretinoin in pregnancy. This risk applies regardless of how mild or brief the exposure.

  • I understand that isotretinoin causes severe, life-threatening birth defects and must never be taken during pregnancy. If I become pregnant during treatment, I will stop isotretinoin immediately and contact my doctor the same day.
Part 3 — Pregnancy Prevention Requirement

I confirm that I have discussed the mandatory pregnancy prevention requirements with my physician, and I agree to meet these requirements throughout my entire course of treatment and for one full month after my last dose.

Please check the option that applies to you:

  • Option A — Two Forms of Contraception: I am sexually active with male partners, or I may become sexually active during treatment. I confirm that I am using two concurrent forms of effective contraception and will continue to do so throughout treatment and for one month after my last dose. My current contraception methods are listed below.
  • Option B — Confirmed Abstinence: I confirm that I am not currently sexually active with male partners and I commit to remaining completely abstinent throughout the full duration of my treatment course and for one full month after my last dose. I understand that if my situation changes, I must contact my doctor before any sexual activity occurs to arrange contraception. I understand that abstinence will be discussed and confirmed at each monthly visit.
  • I understand that I must not share isotretinoin capsules with anyone.
  • I understand that I must not donate blood during treatment or for one month after my final dose, as donated blood could be given to a pregnant person.
  • I understand that effective contraception must continue for a full 30 days after my last dose of isotretinoin before it is safe to attempt pregnancy.
Part 4 — Monthly Pregnancy Testing and Office Visit Requirement

I understand and agree to the following:

  • I will attend a monthly in-office appointment before each prescription is refilled. I understand that my prescription cannot be issued without this visit.
  • I will undergo a pregnancy test at every monthly visit, performed in the office. A negative result is required before the prescription is issued.
  • I understand that two negative pregnancy tests were (or will be) required before my first prescription: one at least 30 days before starting, and one immediately before the first prescription.
  • I agree to contact my doctor immediately if I miss a menstrual period, experience any pregnancy symptoms, or have any unprotected sexual exposure with a male partner during treatment.
  • I understand that if I miss a monthly appointment, my prescription will not be refilled until I attend the office and a negative pregnancy test is documented.
Part 5 — Other Significant Side Effects

I have been informed about the following risks and side effects of isotretinoin and I understand them:

  • Dry lips, skin, eyes, and nasal passages: Very common. I have been told how to manage these with moisturisers, lip balm, and artificial tears.
  • Increased sun sensitivity: I understand I must use SPF 30+ sunscreen daily and protect my skin from sun exposure during treatment.
  • Initial acne flare: A temporary worsening of acne in the first 4–6 weeks is common and does not mean the medication is failing.
  • Mood changes and mental health: I understand that some patients experience depression, anxiety, or mood changes during treatment. I will contact my doctor promptly if I notice changes in my mood, persistent sadness, withdrawal from activities, or any thoughts of self-harm.
  • Blood lipid and liver enzyme changes: I understand that isotretinoin can raise triglycerides, cholesterol, and liver enzymes, and that blood tests are required to monitor this. I agree to limit or avoid alcohol during treatment.
  • Pseudotumour cerebri (increased brain pressure): Rarely, isotretinoin can cause this serious condition. I know to seek immediate medical care if I develop severe headache, visual changes, nausea, or ringing in the ears.
  • Muscle and joint aches: I will notify my doctor if these are significant or limiting.
  • Gastrointestinal symptoms: I will notify my doctor if I develop significant abdominal pain, bloody stools, or persistent diarrhea during treatment.
  • Fragile skin: I understand that waxing, dermabrasion, and certain laser treatments must be avoided during treatment and for at least 6 months after finishing, due to risk of scarring.
  • Hair thinning: Temporary hair thinning is possible. I have been told this typically resolves after treatment ends.
  • Vitamin A toxicity: I must not take additional vitamin A supplements during treatment.
  • Drug interaction: I must not take tetracycline antibiotics (doxycycline, minocycline, tetracycline) concurrently with isotretinoin due to risk of increased brain pressure.

Progesterone-only ("mini") pill: I have been informed that there is a theoretical interaction between isotretinoin and progesterone-only contraceptive pills. If this is my current contraception, I have discussed an alternative or additional method with my doctor.
