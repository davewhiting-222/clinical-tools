Eating Out on a Low Carbohydrate or Ketogenic Diet

Prepared by your family doctor

December 28, 2024

The Good News: You can enjoy dining out while following a low-carb or ketogenic lifestyle. The secret is focusing on the delicious fillings and proteins while leaving behind the starchy "delivery vehicles" like bread, rice, and potatoes.

Two Essential Rules for Success

Rule #1: "Ditch the Wrapper"

Eat the inside of your meal and leave the outside. You can enjoy all the flavors—the beef, cheese, bacon, vegetables, and sauces—but skip the bread, buns, tortillas, and pasta that wrap around them.

Rule #2: "Don't Drink Your Calories"

Liquid sugar goes straight to your liver and converts to fat immediately. Avoid juice, soda, sweet iced tea, energy drinks, flavored coffees, and alcohol. Instead, choose water, sparkling water, black coffee, or unsweetened tea.

Your Restaurant Survival Guide

Here are the exact phrases to use with your server at different types of restaurants:

Restaurant Type What to Order What You'll Enjoy
Burger Joint "Lettuce wrap, no bun, no ketchup" Beef patties, cheese, bacon, pickles, mustard, mayo
Steakhouse "Swap the potato for extra vegetables" Your favorite steak with double green vegetables
Breakfast Diner "Hold the hashbrowns, add tomato slices" Bacon, eggs, sausage, cheese, tomato
Coffee Shop "Americano with heavy cream" Rich coffee without hidden sugars

Simple Swaps for Popular Cuisines

Sandwich and Sub Shops

Order: "Sub in a tub" or meat and cheese roll-ups
You get: All your favorite sandwich fillings—roast beef, turkey, cheese, peppers, onions, oil, and vinegar—served in a bowl without the bread.

Mexican Restaurants

Order: Burrito bowl with extra guacamole
You get: Chicken or beef, cheese, salsa, sour cream, and plenty of guacamole. Skip the tortilla, rice, and beans.

Italian Restaurants

Ask: "Can you serve the meat sauce and meatballs over zucchini noodles or roasted cauliflower?"
Alternative: Simply eat the meatballs and sauce, leaving the pasta behind.

Rotisserie or Deli Counters

Quick Option: Pre-cooked rotisserie chicken (leg or thigh) with a bagged salad and full-fat Caesar or Ranch dressing.

Your Action Plan Checklist

Before You Go:

  • Review the restaurant's menu online if possible
  • Identify 2-3 dishes that can be easily modified
  • Plan your drink order (water, sparkling water, or unsweetened beverages)

When Ordering:

  • Speak confidently—restaurants accommodate dietary requests regularly
  • Ask for substitutions: "Instead of fries, could I have extra vegetables?"
  • Request sauces on the side to control portions

If You're Unsure:

  • Choose simple grilled meats with vegetables
  • Ask how dishes are prepared—avoid breaded or heavily sauced items
  • When in doubt, stick to salads with protein and full-fat dressing

Why This Approach Works

Low-carbohydrate and ketogenic diets work by keeping your body in a state where it burns fat for fuel instead of relying on sugar and starches¹. When you eat out using these principles, you're maintaining the metabolic benefits of your diet while still enjoying social meals and restaurant experiences².

Remember: You're not giving up flavor or satisfaction—you're simply choosing the most nutritious parts of the meal. Most people find they feel more satisfied and have better energy when they focus on protein, healthy fats, and vegetables rather than filling up on bread and starches.

Important: If you have diabetes, are taking medications for blood sugar control, or have other medical conditions, discuss your dietary plans with me before making significant changes. Some medications may need adjustment when following a low-carbohydrate diet³.
