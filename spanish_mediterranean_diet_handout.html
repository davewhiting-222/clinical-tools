Spanish Mediterranean Diet Guide

Prepared by your family doctor December 19, 2024
What is the Spanish Mediterranean Diet?
This is a scientifically-proven approach that combines the heart-healthy Mediterranean eating pattern with principles that promote fat burning and metabolic health. Unlike restrictive diets, this approach emphasizes delicious, whole foods that you can enjoy long-term.

The Four Core Principles

1. Keep Carbohydrates Very Low (Under 30g Daily)

Your carbohydrates should come exclusively from green vegetables and salads. This keeps your blood sugar stable while providing essential fiber and nutrients for digestive health.

Green vegetables to enjoy: Spinach, arugula, kale, watercress, romaine lettuce, butter lettuce, iceberg lettuce, endive, radicchio, bok choy, Swiss chard, collard greens, broccoli, broccolini, Brussels sprouts, cabbage, cauliflower, zucchini, summer squash, cucumbers, bell peppers (all colors), asparagus, green beans, snow peas, snap peas, artichokes, and fresh herbs like basil, parsley, cilantro, and dill.

2. Make Olive Oil Your Primary Fat (30ml Daily Minimum)

Consume at least 2 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil every day. This provides heart-healthy monounsaturated fats that actually help lower bad cholesterol and reduce inflammation throughout your body.

3. Choose Fish as Your Main Protein (4+ Days Weekly)

Focus on fatty fish like salmon, sardines, and mackerel, which provide omega-3 fatty acids that protect your heart and brain. You can also include shellfish, eggs, and lean meats while limiting red meat.

4. Moderate Wine is Permitted

If you choose to drink alcohol, red wine (1-2 glasses daily) fits within this eating pattern and provides antioxidants. Always discuss alcohol consumption with your healthcare provider first.

Why This Approach Works

Clinical Benefits:
Research shows this eating pattern can improve cholesterol levels, reduce triglycerides by over 50%, raise protective HDL cholesterol, and help resolve metabolic syndrome. The combination of Mediterranean foods makes it highly sustainable - studies show 88-92% of people can stick with it long-term.1,2

Sample Daily Menu Options

Breakfast Options:
• Spanish tortilla (eggs only - no potatoes) with herbs and olive oil
• Scrambled eggs with chorizo and spinach
• Smoked salmon with cream cheese and cucumber
• Avocado boats filled with tuna salad
• Greek yogurt with crushed walnuts and olive oil drizzle

Lunch Options:
• Mediterranean salad with anchovies, olives, feta, and olive oil dressing
• Gazpacho (tomato-based, no bread) with hard-boiled eggs
• Grilled sardines with roasted vegetables
• Tuna-stuffed avocados with mixed greens

Dinner Options:
• Paella-style dish (cauliflower rice, seafood, saffron, olive oil)
• Grilled sea bass with ratatouille (no potatoes)
• Lamb chops with rosemary and roasted Brussels sprouts

• Chicken thighs with olives and bell peppers

Beverages: Water, sparkling water, herbal teas, black coffee, red wine (1-2 glasses if desired)

Getting Started: Your First Week Action Plan

  1. Clean out your pantry: Remove grains, sugars, and processed foods to avoid temptation
  2. Stock up on essentials: Buy quality extra virgin olive oil, frozen fish fillets, eggs, and plenty of green vegetables
  3. Plan three simple meals: Start with basic combinations like fish + vegetables + olive oil
  4. Track your olive oil: Measure your 30ml (2 tablespoons) daily to build the habit
  5. Focus on fish: Aim for fish at least 4 times in your first week
  6. Prepare snacks: Pre-portion nuts, prepare hard-boiled eggs, and have olives ready
  7. Stay hydrated: Drink plenty of water, especially in the first few days
Important Considerations:
If you have diabetes, kidney disease, liver disease, or take medications for blood pressure or blood sugar, discuss this eating approach with your healthcare provider before starting. Some people may experience temporary fatigue or digestive changes in the first week as their body adapts.
Remember: This isn't a temporary diet - it's a sustainable way of eating that emphasizes delicious, whole foods. Focus on building habits gradually rather than achieving perfection immediately. The variety of foods available means you'll never feel deprived or bored with your meals.
