Hand Washing: Your Best Defense Against Germs

Prepared by your family doctor
December 28, 2024
Why This Matters: Washing your hands is the single best way to stop germs from spreading. It can prevent many illnesses and keep you and your family healthy.

When Should You Wash Your Hands?

Before:

  • Eating or drinking
  • Making food
  • Touching your face, eyes, nose, or mouth
  • Taking care of someone who is sick

After:

  • Using the washroom
  • Blowing your nose, coughing, or sneezing
  • Touching animals
  • Taking out garbage
  • Being in public places
  • Caring for someone who is sick

How to Wash Your Hands Right

Step What to Do Time
1 Put your hands under clean, running water -
2 Put soap on your wet hands -
3 Rub your hands together to make bubbles 20 seconds
4 Scrub all parts: palms, backs, between fingers, under nails Keep scrubbing
5 Rinse hands well under clean water -
6 Dry with a clean towel or let air dry -
Easy Way to Time 20 Seconds: Sing "Happy Birthday" twice, or count slowly to 20. This gives soap time to kill germs.

What About Hand Sanitizer?

Hand sanitizer is good when you can't wash with soap and water. But soap and water work better.

How to Use Hand Sanitizer:

  1. Use sanitizer with at least 60% alcohol
  2. Put enough to cover all of your hands
  3. Rub hands together until dry (about 20 seconds)
  4. Don't rinse or wipe off before it dries
Remember: Hand sanitizer doesn't work as well on very dirty or greasy hands. Always choose soap and water when you can.

Tips for Your Family

For Kids:

  • Help them reach the sink with a step stool
  • Teach them to sing a song while washing
  • Make it fun with colorful soap
  • Show them how to scrub all parts of their hands

Make It Easier:

  • Keep soap by every sink
  • Have clean towels ready
  • Carry small hand sanitizer when out
  • Set a good example - wash your hands often
Don't Forget: Even with clean hands, try not to touch your face, especially your eyes, nose, and mouth. This is where germs like to enter your body.
