This policy outlines how Dr. David Whiting Inc. manages personal information specifically related to digital communication (SMS) in compliance with the British Columbia Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

1. Consent & Revocation

All digital communication with patients requires explicit consent, which is typically obtained verbally during a clinical consultation. Consent can be revoked at any time. Patients may opt-out of receiving further digital messages by replying "STOP" to any SMS received or by contacting our office directly.

2. Purpose of Communication

We use digital communication solely to deliver requested clinical handouts, dietary guidelines, and educational materials. We never engage in any form of marketing or promotional messaging.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We highly value your privacy. We never share information of any kind with third parties, including mobile numbers or opt-in data, except as strictly required by law or for the direct delivery of the requested service. No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

4. Data Security

We employ standard security measures to ensure the integrity of the data used to facilitate these communications. Information is stored and processed in accordance with BC provincial medical record standards.
