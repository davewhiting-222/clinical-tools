Skin Examination

Consent and Preferences

1  ·  Chest and Breasts

Examining the front of the chest requires full exposure. Removing the bra is recommended — examining over or around it significantly limits what can be seen.

2  ·  Buttocks and Lower Abdomen

Melanoma can occur in these areas. If underwear is kept on, the physician will need to briefly adjust the fabric to view the skin underneath.

3  ·  Inner Thigh

Examining the inner thigh requires adjusting the position of your legs.
